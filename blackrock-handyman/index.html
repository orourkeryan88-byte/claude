About Us

A local team

We're Peter and Alex — a father and son team of Irish carpenters and handymen based in Blackrock, South Dublin. We've been working in and around Dublin homes for years, building a reputation on doing the job properly and treating every customer's home with respect.

Whether it's a single shelf that needs hanging or a full room of flat pack furniture to build, we bring the same care and attention to every job. We turn up when we say we will, we clean up after ourselves, and we don't cut corners.
