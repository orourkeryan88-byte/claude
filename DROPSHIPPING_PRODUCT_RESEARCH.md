## Read this first: the math changed in 2025

The classic "AliExpress item costs $3, sell it for $20, pocket $17" model no longer holds cleanly. In 2025 the US eliminated the **de minimis exemption** (the rule that let parcels under $800 enter duty-free) — first for China/Hong Kong (May 2, 2025), then globally (Aug 29, 2025). As of **Feb 28, 2026**, ad-valorem (percentage-based) duty applies to international postal shipments, with formal customs entry now required. ([Easyship](https://www.easyship.com/blog/end-of-de-minimis-for-china), [GHY International](https://www.ghy.com/trade-compliance/us-de-minimis-exemption-ends-for-china-low-value-imports/), [tariffstool.com](https://www.tariffstool.com/guides/de-minimis-exemption-ended-2026))

A further wrinkle as of **Feb 20, 2026**: the Supreme Court struck down the prior 20% IEEPA tariff layer on China-origin goods (10% reciprocal + 10% fentanyl); it was replaced same-day with a flat **10% Section 122 tariff**. Section 301 tariffs (25–100% depending on category) remain separately in force and may or may not apply to a given HS code. Net effect: a flat **~30% duty placeholder is no longer a safe assumption** — depending on the product's HS code and whether Section 301 applies, all-in duty could be as low as **~10%** or as high as **~45%**. Verify the specific HS code before pricing, and favor suppliers offering **DDP shipping or US-based warehouses** to keep landed cost predictable.

It went from a viral TikTok hook (April 2026 PR coverage) to a flooded multi-brand category within roughly two months. Still profitable on paper (~$13–15/unit gross margin), but not a low-competition entry point.

## Reality check: gross margin ≠ net profit

The margin figures above are **before marketing spend**. If you're running paid ads instead of organic content:

- Average ecommerce CAC: **$68–$84** (2025) ([ringly.io](https://www.ringly.io/blog/ecommerce-customer-acquisition-cost-statistics-2026))
- Meta ads 2025: median CPA **$38.17**, median ROAS for new-customer campaigns **~2.2x** ([ringly.io](https://www.ringly.io/blog/ecommerce-customer-acquisition-cost-statistics-2026))
- TikTok ads 2025: CPA **$32.74**, average ROAS **~1.4x** ([stackmatix.com](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-budget-guide-2026))

A median Meta CPA of $38 exceeds the margin on any single one of these products. Beauty is well-suited to working around this via **organic UGC content** (demo/transformation videos perform natively on TikTok without feeling like an ad).

## Other cost factors baked into "landed cost" above

- AliExpress Standard Shipping to the US: typically **$2–$10**, 15–45 days delivery in 2026 ([dodropshipping.com](https://dodropshipping.com/aliexpress-shipping-times/), [jingsourcing.com](https://jingsourcing.com))
- Payment processing: Shopify Payments/Stripe ≈ **2.9% + $0.30**; PayPal ≈ **3.49% + $0.49** ([trueprofit.io](https://trueprofit.io/blog/how-much-does-shopify-take-per-sale), [synctrack.io](https://synctrack.io/blog/shopify-paypal-fee/))
- Standard markup rule of thumb: **2.5–3x landed cost**, though sources caution this no longer reliably covers costs post-tariff change ([dodropshipping.com](https://dodropshipping.com/dropshipping-pricing-strategy/))

## Data confidence note

AliExpress, Amazon, Alibaba, and TikTok Shop all block direct page scraping (consistent HTTP 403 on automated fetch attempts), so figures throughout are synthesized from Google-indexed listing snippets, wholesale summaries, and third-party price trackers (Alitools, Accio, imall) rather than live-verified listings. Confirm exact pricing, MOQs, and current duty rates with actual suppliers before committing inventory budget.
