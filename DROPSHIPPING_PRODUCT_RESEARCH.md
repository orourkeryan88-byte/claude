# Dropshipping Product Research — June 2026

Goal: source items cheaply from AliExpress, retail at $15–$20+ (higher is better), land roughly **$15 profit per unit before ad spend**.

## Read this first: the math changed in 2025

The classic "AliExpress item costs $3, sell it for $20, pocket $17" model no longer holds cleanly. In 2025 the US eliminated the **de minimis exemption** (the rule that let parcels under $800 enter duty-free) — first for China/Hong Kong (May 2, 2025), then globally (Aug 29, 2025). Through 2025 the duty on small China-origin postal parcels bounced between a flat $100–$200/item and a 30–54% ad-valorem rate; as of **Feb 28, 2026** only percentage-based (ad valorem) duty applies to international postal shipments, with formal customs entry (importer of record, HTS classification) now required. ([Easyship](https://www.easyship.com/blog/end-of-de-minimis-for-china), [GHY International](https://www.ghy.com/trade-compliance/us-de-minimis-exemption-ends-for-china-low-value-imports/), [tariffstool.com](https://www.tariffstool.com/guides/de-minimis-exemption-ended-2026))

Practical takeaways baked into the numbers below:
- I added an estimated **~30% duty** on top of item cost as a placeholder — verify the actual current rate for your product's HS code before pricing, since this has moved fast and can swing your margin by several dollars.
- Favor AliExpress suppliers offering **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) shipping or US-based overseas warehouses** — more sellers now offer this specifically to absorb the customs complexity, which keeps your landed-cost math predictable.
- All three picks below still clear a healthy margin even with duty included, which is why they made the cut over cheaper-looking alternatives that don't.

## 1. Pet Deshedding / Grooming Glove

**Why:** Cheapest viable item in this research, real US retail comps already sit at $15–$25 (Furbliss, HandsOn Gloves), and unlike several other pet/beauty categories researched, no source flagged this as oversaturated — it's treated as a reliable evergreen pet-niche staple. Silicone construction ships flat, can't break, no battery/liquid restrictions.

**Cost breakdown:**
- AliExpress unit cost: $1.50–$3 ([AliExpress deshedding glove listings](https://www.aliexpress.com/w/wholesale-deshedding-glove.html))
- AliExpress Standard Shipping (small/light item): ~$2
- Estimated duty (~30% of item cost): ~$0.45–$0.90
- **Landed cost: ~$4–$6**
- Payment processing (2.9% + $0.30 on a $19.99 sale): ~$0.88
- **Suggested retail: $19.99 → gross profit ≈ $13–$15/unit**

**Demand evidence:** Treated as a steady, evergreen pet-care staple across 2025–2026 sourcing roundups rather than a viral spike — that's a feature here, not a bug, since it means lower boom-bust risk. ([Tradelle, 12 Trending Pet Dropshipping Products 2025–2026](https://www.tradelle.io/blog/12-trending-pet-dropshipping-products-in-2025-for-cats-and-dogs/))

**Sourcing search terms:** "deshedding glove," "pet grooming glove silicone," "cat dog hair remover mitt." Order samples from 2–3 suppliers with 1,000+ orders and 4.7+ rating before committing.

## Reality check: gross margin ≠ net profit

The $13–17/unit figures above are **margin before marketing spend** — i.e., what you keep if the customer finds you for free (organic TikTok/Reels, SEO, word of mouth). If you're running paid ads instead, current benchmarks matter a lot:

- Average ecommerce customer acquisition cost (CAC): **$68–$84** (2025), up ~40% since 2023 ([ringly.io](https://www.ringly.io/blog/ecommerce-customer-acquisition-cost-statistics-2026))
- Meta ads 2025: median CPA **$38.17**, median ROAS for new-customer campaigns **~2.2x** — meaning ad spend alone eats roughly 45% of revenue on a cold-traffic sale ([ringly.io](https://www.ringly.io/blog/ecommerce-customer-acquisition-cost-statistics-2026))
- TikTok ads 2025: CPA **$32.74**, average ROAS **~1.4x** ([stackmatix.com](https://www.stackmatix.com/blog/tiktok-ads-cost-budget-guide-2026))

A median Meta CPA of $38 is larger than the entire margin on any single one of these $20–25 products. **A single-product store running cold paid traffic on one of these items alone will likely lose money per acquisition.** This is normal in dropshipping and is why successful stores rely on one or more of: organic/UGC content (free distribution), bundling 2–3 items to lift average order value above the CAC, retargeting/email flows to push repeat purchases (cheaper than cold acquisition), or a tight niche brand build instead of one-off product testing.

## Other cost factors baked into "landed cost" above

- AliExpress Standard Shipping to the US: typically **$2–$5** for small/light items, 8–20 days delivery ([dodropshipping.com](https://dodropshipping.com/aliexpress-shipping-times/))
- Payment processing: Shopify Payments/Stripe ≈ **2.9% + $0.30**; PayPal ≈ **3.49% + $0.49** (notably higher) ([trueprofit.io](https://trueprofit.io/blog/how-much-does-shopify-take-per-sale), [synctrack.io](https://synctrack.io/blog/shopify-paypal-fee/))
- Standard dropshipping pricing rule of thumb: **2.5–3x landed cost** as a starting markup, though several 2025–2026 sources caution this no longer reliably covers costs post-tariff change ([dodropshipping.com](https://dodropshipping.com/dropshipping-pricing-strategy/), [DSers](https://www.dsers.com/blog/us-tariffs-aliexpress-dropshipping/))

## Data confidence note

AliExpress blocks direct page scraping, so unit-cost figures above are synthesized from Google-indexed listing snippets and dropshipping-blog cost breakdowns rather than live-verified listings. Treat the cost ranges as a starting point — confirm exact pricing and current duty rates with actual suppliers and a customs broker/HTS lookup before committing inventory budget.
