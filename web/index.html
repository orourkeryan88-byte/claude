Does it actually sound real?
Yes — it holds a natural back-and-forth, not a robotic menu. The best way to judge is to ring the demo yourself and hear it answer a call.
Do I have to change my number?
No. You keep your existing number. It simply forwards to Southline when you don't pick up — you can still answer calls yourself any time you're free.
What happens when I'm with a customer?
That's exactly when it earns its keep. The call forwards, Southline answers, books them in, and texts you the details so you can carry on with the job.
How long does setup take?
Usually up and running within a day. We build it around your hours, services and pricing, then you switch on call forwarding in about two minutes.

One simple plan with everything included — no tiers and no add-ons. We tailor the setup to your business, so book a quick chat and we'll give you a straight quote.
