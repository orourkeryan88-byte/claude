SHIPPING & RETURNS

LAST UPDATED: JUNE 2026

We want getting your RELIER order to be as smooth as possible. Here's everything you need to know about delivery and our returns process.

UK SHIPPING

We despatch all in-stock UK orders within 1–2 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding bank holidays). You'll receive a despatch confirmation email once your order is on its way.

SERVICE ESTIMATED DELIVERY COST
Standard Delivery 3–5 business days £4.99
Express Delivery 1–2 business days £7.99
Free Standard Delivery 3–5 business days FREE on orders over £100

Delivery times are estimates and begin from the date of despatch, not the date of order. We are not responsible for delays caused by the courier or circumstances outside our control.

INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING

We ship to most countries worldwide. International shipping costs and delivery times vary depending on your location and are calculated at checkout.

DESTINATION ESTIMATED DELIVERY COST
Europe (EU) 5–10 business days From £12.99
USA & Canada 7–14 business days From £14.99
Rest of World 10–21 business days From £17.99

Please note that international orders may be subject to import duties, taxes, and customs fees imposed by your country's government. These charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in our shipping costs. We are unable to predict these charges and recommend checking with your local customs authority before placing an order.

For EU and international customers: goods are shipped from the United Kingdom. You may need to pay VAT or import duties upon delivery. Customs processing may cause delays beyond our estimated delivery times.

RETURNS

We want you to love your RELIER order. If for any reason you are not completely satisfied, we accept returns within 14 days of the delivery date.

To be eligible for a return, your item must be:

  • Unworn and unused
  • In its original condition
  • With all tags still attached
  • In its original or equivalent packaging

Items that have been worn, washed, damaged, or altered cannot be returned unless they are faulty. Sale items are eligible for return under the same conditions.

HOW TO INITIATE A RETURN

To start a return, please follow these steps:

  • Email us at hello@relier.co.uk with your order number and the reason for your return.
  • We will respond within 1–2 business days with return instructions and a return address.
  • Pack your item securely and send it to the address provided. We recommend using a tracked service as we cannot be held responsible for items lost in transit.
  • Once we receive your return and confirm it meets our return conditions, we will process your refund or exchange.

Please do not send items back without contacting us first — unrequested returns may not be processed.

REFUNDS

Once we have received and inspected your returned item, we will send you an email to notify you that your refund has been approved or rejected.

If approved, your refund will be processed within 5–7 business days of receiving the return. Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout. Please allow additional time for your bank or card provider to post the refund to your account — this can take up to 10 business days depending on your financial institution.

We will refund the full price of the goods and the standard delivery cost (if you paid for delivery). We do not refund the cost of return postage unless the item is faulty or we made an error with your order.

EXCHANGES

We are happy to exchange items for a different size or colour, subject to availability. To request an exchange, please email us at hello@relier.co.uk with your order number and the details of the exchange you would like.

As exchanges depend on stock availability, we recommend contacting us as soon as possible to check availability before returning your item. If your preferred exchange item is out of stock, we will issue a full refund instead.

FAULTY ITEMS

If you receive an item that is faulty, damaged, or not as described, please contact us within 30 days of delivery at hello@relier.co.uk with your order number, a description of the fault, and clear photographs. We will arrange a replacement or full refund, including return postage costs, as quickly as possible.

QUESTIONS?

If you have any questions about your order, shipping, or returns, please don't hesitate to get in touch:

  • Email: hello@relier.co.uk

We aim to respond to all enquiries within 1–2 business days.
