# Template 07 — Service Agreement Skeleton

> ⚠️ **Not legal advice.** This is a structure to hand to a solicitor/attorney in your jurisdiction before you use it with a real client. Consumer, contract, and data-protection law vary by country.

**Parties:** RELIER ("Agency") and {{CLIENT LEGAL NAME}} ("Client").
**Effective date:** {{DATE}}

### 1. Services
Agency will provide the services in Schedule A. Anything not listed in Schedule A is out of scope and quoted separately.

### 2. Schedule A — Scope (be specific; vagueness here causes every future dispute)
- Channels managed: {{e.g. Meta Ads, Google Ads}}
- Ad spend under management: up to {{$X}}/month
- Creative included: {{X static + X video concepts per month}}
- Landing pages: {{X per quarter}}
- Reporting: weekly written update + monthly strategy call
- Named excluded: SEO, email deliverability remediation, website development, PR, organic social management {{adjust}}

### 3. Fees
- Management fee: {{$X}} per month, invoiced on the {{Nth}}, payable within {{7/14}} days.
- Onboarding/setup fee: {{$X}}, due on signature.
- Performance component (optional): {{X% of tracked revenue / $X per qualified lead}}, defined and measured as {{definition}}.
- Ad spend is paid by Client directly to the platforms and is not Agency revenue.
- Late payment: work may pause after {{X}} days overdue; {{X}}% interest as permitted by law.

### 4. Term and termination
- Initial term: {{90 days}} — chosen because paid media needs a full learning and optimisation cycle to be judged fairly.
- Then rolling monthly, terminable by either party on {{30}} days' written notice.
- Immediate termination for material breach, non-payment, or illegality.
- On termination: Agency delivers all assets, removes its access, and provides a handover document within 5 business days. Client keeps ownership of all ad accounts, creative, and data.

### 5. Client responsibilities
Timely access, approvals within {{2}} business days, accurate information, funded ad accounts, and compliance of Client's products and claims with applicable law.

### 6. Intellectual property
Creative produced under this agreement transfers to Client on full payment. Agency retains its own frameworks, templates, tooling, and internal processes. Agency may reference the work in its portfolio unless Client opts out in writing.

### 7. Confidentiality
Mutual. Survives termination by {{2}} years.

### 8. Data protection
Each party complies with applicable data protection law ({{GDPR/CCPA/etc.}}). Where Agency processes personal data on Client's behalf, the Data Processing Addendum in Schedule B applies.

### 9. No guarantee of results
Agency will apply professional skill and care. Advertising outcomes depend on factors outside Agency's control — platform policy, auction dynamics, Client's offer, pricing, sales follow-up, and market conditions. No specific result is guaranteed. (Any performance promises must be provable; do not put a revenue guarantee in writing that you cannot honour.)

### 10. Liability
Agency's aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid in the {{3}} months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Nothing excludes liability that cannot be excluded by law.

### 11. Non-solicitation
Neither party solicits the other's staff for {{12}} months after termination.

### 12. General
Governing law {{jurisdiction}}. Entire agreement. Variations in writing. Signature blocks below.
