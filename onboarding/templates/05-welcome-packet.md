## What we need from you
1. **Approvals within 2 business days.** Creative and copy sitting in a queue is the single biggest cause of slow results.
2. **Speed on leads.** Contact every inbound lead within 5 minutes during business hours. Lead response speed moves conversion more than any targeting change we can make.
3. **Honest numbers.** Tell us what actually closed. Platform data lies; your bank account doesn't.
4. **One decision-maker.** Committee approvals kill momentum.
5. **Raw material.** Photos, videos, testimonials, product footage. The account is only as good as the creative we have to feed it.

## What you can expect from us
- A written weekly update whether the news is good or bad.
- No jargon in reports — spend, leads, cost per lead, sales, return.
- We tell you when something isn't working before you have to ask.
- We recommend against spending when spending isn't the answer.

## Timeline reality check
- **Weeks 1–2:** setup, tracking, creative, launch. No meaningful performance data yet.
