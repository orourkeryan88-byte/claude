# Template 02 — Client Intake Questionnaire

> Send as a form (Google Forms / Typeform / Tally). Target: 15–20 minutes. Anything longer and it doesn't come back.

## Section 1 — Business basics
1. Legal business name and trading name
2. Website URL(s)
3. What do you sell, in one sentence, as a customer would say it?
4. Years in business, team size, current annual revenue band
5. Primary contact for approvals (name, email, phone) — who has final sign-off?
6. Who else must be copied on decisions?

## Section 2 — The offer
7. List your products/services with price points
8. What is your single most profitable offer?
9. What is your average order value / average contract value?
10. What is your gross margin on that offer?
11. What's your customer lifetime value, and how did you calculate it?
12. Is there a guarantee, trial, or risk-reversal? Describe it.
13. What's the #1 objection people raise before buying?

## Section 3 — The customer
14. Describe your best customer. Who are they, what job do they hold, what triggers them to look for you?
15. What are they using instead of you right now (including "nothing")?
16. Where do they spend time online?
17. Paste 3–5 real customer quotes, reviews, or support tickets. Verbatim. This is gold — don't paraphrase.

## Section 4 — Numbers and targets
18. Current monthly marketing spend, by channel
19. Current cost per lead and cost per acquisition
20. Current monthly leads / sales volume
21. Website traffic per month and main sources
22. Email list size and average open rate
23. **What number defines success in 90 days?** Be specific: "35 qualified calls/month at under $180 each" beats "more leads."
24. What's the maximum you can pay to acquire a customer and still be happy?

## Section 5 — What's been tried
25. Channels you've run before and what happened
26. Agencies/freelancers you've worked with — what went wrong?
27. What's working right now that we should not touch?
28. Anything that is off-limits (claims, channels, competitors, creative styles)?

## Section 6 — Sales and fulfilment
29. What happens after a lead comes in? Walk through it step by step.
30. Who follows up, how fast, and on what channel?
31. What's your lead-to-sale close rate?
32. What's your capacity ceiling — how many new customers can you handle per month before quality drops?

## Section 7 — Brand and assets
33. Logo, fonts, colours, brand guidelines — link
34. Existing photo/video assets — link
35. Testimonials, case studies, before/afters — link
36. Tone of voice: pick 3 words
37. Compliance/regulatory constraints (finance, health, legal claims)

## Section 8 — Practicalities
38. Who can approve creative, and how fast?
39. Preferred meeting day/time and timezone
40. Anything else we should know that we didn't ask?

---

### Internal scoring after intake returns
Rate 1–5 each: **Offer strength · Margin headroom · Data quality · Sales follow-up speed · Creative asset supply.**
Any score of 1–2 becomes a fix in Week 1 of the roadmap — do not launch traffic into a broken downstream.
