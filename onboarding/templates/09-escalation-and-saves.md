# Template 09 — Churn Signals, Escalation, and the Save Play

## Early warning signals (check weekly)
| Signal | Severity |
|---|---|
| Approvals slipping past 5 days | Medium |
| Client stops replying in the channel | High |
| A new stakeholder appears asking "what are we paying for?" | High |
| Client asks for a month-by-month breakdown of hours | High |
| Missed two monthly calls in a row | High |
| Results flat for 6+ weeks with no test velocity | Critical |
| Client mentions "reviewing options" or "internal restructure" | Critical |

## The save play (run within 48 hours of a Critical signal)
1. **Call, don't email.** Account lead phones the decision-maker. No agenda deck.
2. **Ask, then shut up.** "Be straight with me — where is this falling short?" Take notes. Do not defend.
3. **Reflect it back in writing** the same day: what you heard, unedited.
4. **Bring a plan within 3 business days:** three specific changes, dates, and the number each should move. Include something you'll do at no charge if the fault is yours.
5. **Set a 30-day checkpoint** with an explicit success definition — and honour it, including walking away if it isn't met.

## When to fire a client
- They ask you to make claims that aren't true.
- Abuse toward your team. No revenue justifies it.
- Chronic non-payment.
- Their offer or fulfilment is broken and they refuse to fix it — no amount of traffic saves that, and the failure will be attributed to you.

Exit cleanly: written notice, full handover, access removed, final invoice, no bad-mouthing. Ex-clients refer.

## Renewal and expansion
- **Day 60:** ask for the testimonial while the win is fresh.
- **Day 75:** present the next-quarter roadmap *before* renewal comes up, so renewal is a formality about the plan, not a referendum on the past.
- **Expansion triggers:** the primary KPI is being hit consistently, capacity headroom exists, and a second channel has a clear thesis. Never upsell into an underperforming account.
