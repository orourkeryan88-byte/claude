# Template 04 — Kickoff Call Agenda (60 minutes)

**Attendees:** client decision-maker (required), client marketing/sales lead, RELIER account lead + strategist.
**Pre-read:** completed intake form. If it isn't returned, reschedule — do not run kickoff blind.

| Time | Item | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Introductions and roles | Who decides, who executes, who to call |
| 0:05–0:15 | Play back their business to them | Prove we read the intake; correct our assumptions early |
| 0:15–0:25 | The offer under a microscope | Price, margin, guarantee, objections. Most campaigns fail here, not in the ad account |
| 0:25–0:35 | The number that matters | Agree one primary KPI, one target, one date |
| 0:35–0:45 | Customer journey walkthrough | From first click to money in the bank, including sales follow-up |
| 0:45–0:52 | Constraints | Budget, capacity ceiling, compliance, brand no-gos |
| 0:52–0:58 | The next 14 days | Who does what by when |
| 0:58–1:00 | Confirm cadence | Weekly update day, monthly call slot, escalation path |

## Six questions to always ask out loud
1. If we could only move one number in 90 days, which one and why?
2. What would make you say in six months that this was the best money you spent?
3. What would make you fire us?
4. Who inside your business is sceptical about hiring an agency?
5. What's the fastest you can turn around creative approval — honestly?
6. If we brought you 3× the leads next month, what breaks?

## Within 24 hours after the call
Send a written recap: agreed primary KPI + target, confirmed budget, decisions made, open questions, action list with owners and dates. Silence on a recap counts as agreement — say so in the email.
