# RELIER — Client Onboarding System

Everything needed to take a signed client from contract to first campaign live in 14 days.

## The 5 phases

| Phase | When | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Close | Day 0 | Sales | Signed agreement + deposit paid |
| 1. Welcome | Day 0–1 | Account Lead | Welcome email, intake form sent, kickoff booked |
| 2. Discovery | Day 2–5 | Strategist | Intake returned, access granted, audit complete |
| 3. Strategy | Day 6–10 | Strategist | Funnel map, offer, 90-day roadmap approved |
| 4. Launch | Day 11–14 | Media Buyer / Creative | Tracking live, creative shipped, campaigns on |
| 5. Operate | Day 15+ | Account Lead | Weekly reporting, monthly strategy call |

## Files

**Send to the client**
- `templates/01-welcome-email.md` — first email after signature
- `templates/02-client-intake-questionnaire.md` — the discovery form
- `templates/03-access-checklist.md` — what accounts we need and how to grant them
- `templates/04-kickoff-agenda.md` — 60-minute kickoff call
- `templates/05-welcome-packet.md` — how we work, comms, escalation, expectations
- `frameworks/sales-funnel-blueprint.md` — the funnel we build for them
- `frameworks/90-day-roadmap.md` — what happens in month 1, 2, 3

**Run internally**
- `templates/06-onboarding-checklist.md` — the 14-day operational checklist
- `templates/07-service-agreement.md` — scope/terms skeleton (have a lawyer review)
- `frameworks/offer-and-messaging.md` — offer construction + message-market fit
- `frameworks/kpi-and-reporting.md` — the numbers we report and the targets

- `templates/08-monthly-report.md` — the monthly report structure
- `templates/09-escalation-and-saves.md` — churn signals and the save play
