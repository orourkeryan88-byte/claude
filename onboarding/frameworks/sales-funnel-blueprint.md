# The RELIER Sales Funnel Blueprint

The funnel we build for every client. Five stages, one job each. Most agencies obsess over stage 2 and lose money at stages 4 and 5.

```
ATTENTION  →  INTEREST  →  CONVERSION  →  FOLLOW-UP  →  EXPANSION
  (traffic)    (capture)     (offer)      (nurture)     (LTV)
```

---

## Stage 1 — Attention
**Job:** put the right message in front of the right person at the right cost.

- **Cold traffic:** paid social (Meta, TikTok), paid search (Google, Bing), YouTube, organic content.
- **Warm traffic:** retargeting site visitors, video viewers, engagers, email non-openers.
- **Hot traffic:** brand search, existing list, referrals.

**Budget split to start:** 70% cold / 20% warm / 10% hot. Move toward warm/hot as the pixel matures.

**Metrics:** CPM, CTR, cost per click, hook rate (3s video views ÷ impressions), hold rate.
**Diagnostic:** low CTR = creative or audience problem, not a bidding problem.

---

## Stage 2 — Interest (capture)
**Job:** trade something of genuine value for contact details, or move them straight to the offer.

Pick one entry point — never two on the same page:
| Entry point | Best for | Typical conversion |
|---|---|---|
| Lead magnet (guide, checklist, calculator) | High-consideration B2B/services | 20–40% |
| Quiz / assessment | Products with variety or diagnosis | 30–50% |
| Free trial / sample | SaaS, subscription, ecom | 5–15% |
| Book-a-call | High ticket ($3k+) | 2–8% of clicks |
| Direct-to-product | Low-ticket ecom, impulse | 1–4% |

**Landing page anatomy (in order):**
1. Headline — the outcome, not the product
2. Subhead — who it's for, and the mechanism
3. Proof above the fold — a number, a logo row, or a face
4. Three benefit blocks written in customer language
5. Social proof: testimonials with names and specifics
6. Offer stack — what they get, itemised
7. Risk reversal — guarantee, no-card trial, "no pitch on the call"
8. FAQ that kills the top five objections
9. One call to action, repeated 3–4 times, never competing with a second one

**Metrics:** landing page conversion rate, cost per lead, form abandonment.
**Diagnostic:** clicks are cheap but leads are expensive = message-to-page mismatch. The ad promised something the page doesn't deliver.

---

## Stage 3 — Conversion (the offer)
**Job:** make buying the obvious next step.

**The offer equation:**
> Value = (Dream outcome × Perceived likelihood of achieving it) ÷ (Time to result × Effort required)

Improve any of the four. Most businesses only ever discount price, which is the one lever that damages the business.

**Conversion assets:**
- Sales page or sales call script
- Booking page with reminders (SMS + email) — no-shows destroy high-ticket funnels
- Checkout: guest checkout, wallets, trust badges, 3 fields max
- Order bump (+10–30% AOV) and one-click upsell (+15–40% AOV)
- Abandonment recovery: 3 emails + 1 SMS over 72 hours

**Metrics:** lead-to-sale rate, show rate, close rate, AOV, cost per acquisition.
**Diagnostic:** leads are cheap but sales don't happen = offer or sales-process problem. Do not fix this with more traffic.

---

## Stage 4 — Follow-up (where most money is left)
**Job:** convert the 97% who weren't ready today.

**Speed rule:** contact inbound leads within 5 minutes. Conversion drops off a cliff after that — this single change routinely outperforms any targeting work.

**Sequences to build:**
- **New lead (7 emails / 14 days):** deliver → story → objection #1 → proof → objection #2 → offer → deadline
- **No-show recovery:** immediate rebook link, then day 1, day 3
- **Post-purchase (5 emails):** confirm → set expectations → onboarding → ask for review → cross-sell
- **Long nurture:** weekly value email, monthly offer

**Metrics:** speed-to-lead, contact rate, sequence-attributed revenue, list growth.

---

## Stage 5 — Expansion
**Job:** make each customer worth more, so you can afford to pay more to acquire the next one.

Referral ask at the moment of peak satisfaction · Reactivation campaigns to dormant buyers · Subscription or retainer conversion · Cross-sell based on purchase history · Win-back at 60/90/180 days.

**Metrics:** repeat purchase rate, LTV, referral rate, LTV:CAC ratio.
**Target:** LTV:CAC of 3:1 or better. Below 2:1 the business cannot scale on paid media, and no amount of media buying skill fixes that.

## Funnel diagnostic table

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| High CPM, low CTR | Weak creative / wrong audience | New hooks, new angles |
| High CTR, low page conversion | Ad-to-page mismatch | Match headline to ad promise |
| Good leads, no sales | Offer or sales follow-up | Fix speed-to-lead and the offer, not the ads |
| Sales but no profit | Economics broken | Raise price, raise AOV, or cut CAC |
| Works then dies after 3 weeks | Creative fatigue | Refresh creative on a schedule, not on panic |
