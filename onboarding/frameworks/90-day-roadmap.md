# The 90-Day Client Roadmap

Present this at the strategy handoff (Day 6–10). Adapt the specifics; keep the shape.

## Month 1 — Foundation and launch
**Theme:** stop the leaks, then turn on the tap.

| Week | Focus | Deliverables | Success looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Discovery & audit | Intake scored, access verified, tracking audit, competitor scan, customer language swipe file | We know the real numbers, not the assumed ones |
| 2 | Strategy & build | Funnel map, offer brief, economics sheet, 90-day plan approved | Client has signed off on the plan and the target CPA |
| 3 | Tracking & creative | GA4/GTM/pixels/server-side live and tested, landing page built, first creative batch (3 concepts × 3 variants) | A test lead reaches the client's CRM |
| 4 | Launch & stabilise | Campaigns live, budget pacing correct, daily monitoring | Data flowing, no wasted spend, first cost-per-lead reading |

**End of Month 1 milestone:** campaigns live, tracking trustworthy, a baseline cost per lead established. *Not* profitability — anyone promising that in month one is guessing.

## Month 2 — Learn and optimise
**Theme:** find what works and pour into it.

| Week | Focus | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Creative testing round 2 | 3 new angles based on week 4 data |
| 6 | Landing page optimisation | A/B test on headline and offer presentation |
| 7 | Audience & structure | Consolidate winners, cut losers, expand lookalikes/keywords |
| 8 | Follow-up build | Lead nurture sequence, no-show recovery, speed-to-lead fix in the client's process |

**End of Month 2 milestone:** cost per lead at or below target, at least two proven creative angles, follow-up automation live.

## Month 3 — Scale and prove
**Theme:** turn a working funnel into a profitable system.

| Week | Focus | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | Scale winners | Budget increases in controlled steps (20–30% every 3 days, not doubles) |
| 10 | Channel expansion test | Second channel with a stated thesis and a capped test budget |
| 11 | LTV work | Post-purchase sequence, referral ask, reactivation campaign |
| 12 | Review & next quarter | Full 90-day report, what we learned, quarter-two roadmap |

**End of Month 3 milestone:** target CPA hit consistently, LTV:CAC ≥ 3:1, a documented playbook of what works for this business.

## Reporting rhythm
- **Weekly (written, every {{DAY}}):** spend, results, cost per result, what we did, what's next, what's blocked.
- **Monthly (call, 45–60 min):** the report, decisions needed, next month's three priorities.
- **Day 30 / 60 / 90 reviews:** against the milestones above, in writing.

## What we ask the client to commit to
1. Approvals inside 2 business days.
2. 5-minute response to inbound leads during business hours.
3. Truthful sales outcome data every month.
4. A steady supply of raw creative material.
5. Holding the line for the full 90 days before judging the channel.
