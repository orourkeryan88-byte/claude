# Offer Construction & Message-Market Fit

## 1. Mine the language before you write a word
Sources: reviews (theirs and competitors'), support tickets, sales call recordings, Reddit/forums, the intake questionnaire's verbatim quotes.

Tag every line into one of four buckets: **Pain · Desire · Objection · Vocabulary.** Your ad copy is assembled from these, not invented.

## 2. Build the offer
Start from the equation: `Value = (Dream outcome × Perceived likelihood) ÷ (Time × Effort)`

Then stack it:
- **Core deliverable** — what they're actually buying
- **Bonuses that remove obstacles** — each bonus should kill a specific reason not to buy
- **Risk reversal** — guarantee, trial, pilot, "we'll refund if X"
- **Urgency/scarcity that is true** — cohort start dates, capacity limits, seasonal. Fake scarcity is a short-term gain and a long-term brand cost.

## 3. Angles, not adjectives
An angle is a reason to care, not a description. For any product, write at least five:
1. **Problem/agitate** — name the pain sharply
2. **Mechanism** — "the reason this works when other things didn't"
3. **Identity** — "for people who…"
4. **Comparison** — versus the obvious alternative
5. **Proof-led** — a specific result, a specific customer
6. **Contrarian** — challenge a belief the market holds

Test angles before you test colours, thumbnails, or buttons. Angle is worth 10× the delta of any design tweak.

## 4. The one-page message brief (fill per client)
- **Who:** the customer in one sentence, in their words
- **Problem:** the pain they'd describe to a friend
- **Promise:** the outcome, specific and time-bound
- **Mechanism:** why it works
- **Proof:** the three strongest pieces of evidence
- **Objections:** top five and the counter for each
- **Call to action:** one, unambiguous

## 5. Headline formulas that survive contact with reality
- `Get [outcome] in [timeframe] without [the thing they dread]`
- `The [category] for [specific person] who [specific situation]`
- `[Number] [customers] used this to [result]`
- `Stop [painful activity]. Start [desired state].`
