How to work this list

  1. Verify, then record. Open each site, confirm the number, and screen-record 90 seconds while you're there: their page, a competitor running ads properly, one thing you'd change.
  2. Send with no pitch. "Made you this, no pitch — happy to just send it over if it's useful." The ones who reply are pre-sold.
  3. Call the mobile-only ones first. A mobile number and a Gmail address means the owner answers his own phone. No gatekeeper, no committee, decision in a day.
  4. Check the Meta Ad Library for each firm before you call. If they're running nothing, that's your whole opening. If they are, you'll know what they've already tried.
  5. Ask about capacity in the first two minutes. Every firm here can sell a €25k–€60k job. Not all of them can take on more work — and selling leads to a firm with no capacity is how you lose a client in month two.
  6. One firm per patch. Greenoak works D14/D16, Attic Living works Drogheda, Noel Dempsey works from Rathnew. Signing two competitors in one area costs you both.

One legal note

Most of these are limited companies, where B2B electronic marketing with a clear opt-out is standard practice. Some are sole traders, and under Irish and EU rules (GDPR plus ePrivacy) sole traders get treated more like individuals for unsolicited electronic marketing. Phone calls to a listed business number and personalised, opt-out-able email are the safe lane; bulk unsolicited email is not. If you scale this into a real outbound operation, spend an hour with an advisor.
