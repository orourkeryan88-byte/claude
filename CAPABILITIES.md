# Southline AI Receptionist — What it can do

A 24/7 AI receptionist for tradespeople, clinics and salons. It answers every call, books appointments, captures every lead, and routes the rest — for a fraction of the cost of hiring.

---

## On the call
- **Answers every call, 24/7/365** — picks up on the first ring, never misses one.
- **Sounds human** — natural voice, understands accents, handles interruptions; callers often don't realise it's AI.
- **Branded to the business** — greets in their name and knows their hours, services, pricing and FAQs.
- **Follows a real reception workflow** — greet → identify the reason → understand → act → confirm → close.
- **Handles every call type** — new booking, reschedule, cancel, general question, pricing, complaint, message, sales enquiry, emergency, "speak to a person."
- **Answers questions** — hours, services, prices, location, parking, payment methods, guarantees, areas covered, "how soon can you come."
- **Never guesses** — if it doesn't know, it takes a message rather than invent an answer; reads phone numbers back to confirm.

## Booking & calendar
- **Checks real availability** and offers open slots.
- **Checks a specific requested time** — "can I get in tomorrow at 10?" → "yes, that's free, shall I book it?"
- **Handles clashes** — if 10 is taken: "I have 11 that same morning — would that suit, or is there another time that works for you?"
- **Books straight into Google Calendar** and confirms the time back.
- **Qualifies the booking** — appointment type, location, provider, special requests — and submits a structured booking record.

## After every call
- **Texts the owner** the lead instantly (urgent calls flagged ⚠).
- **Logs the lead** to the CRM.
- **Creates the calendar event** and **texts the caller** a confirmation.
- **Follows up** with a text the next day if they didn't book.
- **Daily summary** to the owner each evening (total / booked / urgent / call-backs).
- **Transfers to a human** for emergencies or on request.

## CRM dashboard
- Tracks **every call** — who called, the problem, who to call back, urgency, status.
- **Table + drag-and-drop pipeline** (New → To call back → Booked → Closed).
- **Appointments view**, call-detail notes, **one-tap call-back**, search/sort/filter, and a live feed from the phone line.

## For the agency (reselling)
- **One template → any client** in ~10 minutes.
- **One system serves every client**; leads auto-route to the right owner.
- **Fully rebrandable** — name, hours, services, pricing, FAQs, voice.
- **Cheaper than hiring** — a fraction of a €25–30k/yr receptionist, handling unlimited calls at once.

## To sell it
- **Live, offline-capable demo** in phone-call format — it talks, books, transfers, and offers alternatives.
- **Industry scenarios** (plumber, dentist, salon, vet, clinic, generic) + **rebrand live** to a prospect's name.
- **Export a deployable config** for any prospect on the spot.
- **Missed-call ROI calculator** + pricing page to close.
