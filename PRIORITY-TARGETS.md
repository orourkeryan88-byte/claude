These three are flagged as HUGE sell points. Work them differently from normal
prospects — they're group/enterprise plays, not one-shop sales.

## 1. Smiles Dental — Dún Laoghaire · 01 907 3320 (3 Upper George's St)
## 2. Smiles Dental — Dundrum · 01 525 0800 (Dundrum Town Centre)

**Why they're huge:** Smiles Dental is Bupa-owned with a NETWORK of practices
across Ireland (Dublin, Cork, Galway and more). Both south-Dublin branches were
verified hiring receptionists (as of 6 Jul 2026 checks).

**The play:**
- Land ONE branch (either one) at normal pricing — €350 + €80/mo.
- Deliver hard for 30 days: transcripts, after-hours saves, booking rate.
- Then pitch the group with the case study: "Your Dún Laoghaire branch stopped
  missing calls for €80 a month. Want the same across every practice?"
- A group deal = 15-20+ branches × €80/mo ≈ €1,200-1,600 MRR from ONE relationship.
- Decision-maker for the group won't be at the branch — the branch win is the
  door-opener to Bupa/Smiles head office.

## 3. Blackrock Clinic — Blackrock · 01 283 2222 (Rock Road)

**Why they're huge:** flagship private hospital (Blackrock Health group — also
Galway Clinic, Hermitage, Limerick). Massive daily call volume; they were a top
Indeed hirer for medical receptionists.

**The play:**
- Do NOT pitch replacing their reception team — pitch OVERFLOW + AFTER-HOURS:
  "Every call your desk can't take in 3 rings rolls to the AI — nothing goes
  to voicemail, ever."
- This is an enterprise contract: custom pricing, not €80/mo. Anchor higher
  (hundreds/mo per department) — they spend more on a single locum shift.
- A Blackrock Health logo is the credibility unlock for every clinic in Dublin.
- Longer sales cycle; get the name of the Patient Services / Ops manager, not
  reception.
