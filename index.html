Our Story

The most beloved corner of Temple Bar

Tucked into the cobblestones of East Essex Street, Stage Door has been feeding Dublin's artists, night owls, tourists, and locals for years. It has no neon sign, no PR budget, and absolutely no pretension — just brilliant food, warm chaos, and Alan on the door.

Alan is the kind of host who'll hand you a live music recommendation before you've ordered, remember your name the next morning, and deliver a one-liner so good you'll be repeating it at dinner parties for years. Half of those 4.8-star reviews are really about him.

Inside, the walls are lined with Al Pacino headshots, the seating is mismatched in the best charity-shop tradition, and the intimate booths make every breakfast feel like a secret. There's outdoor seating for Temple Bar's rare sunny moments, and a crowd that tends to lean artistic, creative, and unapologetically themselves.

Stage Door is proudly LGBTQ+ welcoming. Everyone is here. Everyone belongs.
