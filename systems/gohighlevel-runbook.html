Part 1

What the words mean

Just reading · 3 min

HighLevel uses five words a lot. Here is what each one actually means.

A contact

A person.

That's the whole thing. Dave the attic fitter is a contact.

A tag

A sticker you put on a person.

The sticker does nothing by itself. It's just a label so you can find people later.

You stick confirmed-yes on Dave when he texts back. On the day of the call, you search for everyone without that sticker. Those are the ones who might not show up. You ring them.
A custom field

A box where you write down a fact about someone.

HighLevel already gives you boxes for name, phone and email. A custom field is an extra box you make yourself.

"Average job: €28,000" goes in a box you made. His phone number goes in a box that was already there.
A pipeline

A row of columns, like a scoreboard.

Every person gets a card. You slide their card from column to column as things move along.

Dave's card starts in "New Lead". He books a call, so you slide it to "Call Booked". He signs, so you slide it to "Contracted".
A workflow

This is the only tricky one. Think of a doorbell.

When the doorbell rings, you do things in order. Answer the door. Say hello. Put the kettle on.

A workflow is exactly that. One thing starts it. Then jobs happen, one after another.

When Dave books a call → send him an email → wait 5 minutes → send him a text → put a sticker on him.

Two more words you'll see on screen

HighLevel calls the doorbell a trigger. It calls each job an action. That's all those words mean.

This is what you'll be looking at.
One thing that confuses everybody

Waiting is a job too.

Say you want the text to go 5 minutes after the email. You do not set that on the text. You add a box in between that says "Wait 5 minutes".

Email box. Then wait box. Then text box. Three boxes, in a line.

    Read this bit twice. There are two kinds of waiting.

    Time Delay means "wait 5 minutes from right now."
    Appointment Time means "wait until 24 hours before his call."

    You want the second one here. Here's why. If someone books a call for later the same day, "wait 24 hours" from now would land after the call already happened. So it never sends. Appointment Time counts backwards from the call, so it always works.

Part 3

The rest, another day

Same steps, different words in the boxes

You already know how. Every one below is: Automation → Workflows → + Create Workflow → Start from Scratch → name it → pick the doorbell → click + for each job → flip to Publish → Save.

Do them in this order. The first three take about ten minutes each.

1. Catch the people who reply

Doorbell
Customer Replied

Jobs

  1. Add an If/Else — set it to: message contains Y or YES
  2. On the yes side: Add Contact Tag → confirmed-yes
  3. On the yes side: Internal Notification to yourself
  4. On the no side: Internal Notification saying "someone replied, go read it"

Why: on the morning of a call, search for everyone without the confirmed-yes sticker. Those are the ones likely to no-show. Ring them.

2. Know who watched your video

Doorbell
Trigger Link Clicked → pick your video link

Jobs

  1. Add Contact Tag → vsl-watched
  2. Internal Notification → "Dave just watched the video"

You need to make that link first: Marketing → Trigger Links. Then use that link in your emails instead of the normal one.

3. Chase people who didn't show up

Doorbell
Appointment Status → set it to No Show → filter to your Sales Call calendar

Jobs

  1. Add Contact Tag → stage-noshow
  2. Wait 10 minutes → Send SMS
  3. Wait 3 hours → Send Email

Onboarding call reminders.
This is the exact same thing you built in Part 2, just for a different calendar. So don't build it again — go to your Workflows list, find the three dots next to "Sales Call Reminders", and click Clone. Then change the calendar and soften the wording. Two minutes.

If something doesn't work

Open the workflow. Click Execution Logs at the top. It shows you every person who went in, and exactly which step they got stuck on.

Nine times out of ten it's one of three things. It's still on Draft. Or the doorbell had no filter on it. Or a Wait was set to Time Delay when it should have been Appointment Time.
