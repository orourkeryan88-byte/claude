RELIER · HighLevel runbook

96 steps, in order, start to finish

Open HighLevel beside this and work down. Every step is one action. Ticks save in this browser, so you can stop anywhere and the Resume button brings you back.

0 / 96
Step 0 — do this before step 1

Buy an SMS-capable Irish +353 mobile number, text yourself from it, and reply to that text. If the reply doesn't arrive in HighLevel, buy a UK +44 mobile instead and test again. Roughly a third of the steps below depend on two-way SMS working. If neither works, stop and tell me — I'll rebuild the sequences around email and click-tracking.

Session A — Foundations

Steps 1–14 · ~45 min

Nothing here is interesting and all of it is load-bearing. Get the timezone wrong and every reminder in the build fires at the wrong hour.

  1. 1
    Create the sub-account
    Name it RELIER — Internal. Build everything here, never in the agency view.
    Agency view → Sub-Accounts → Create Sub-Account
  2. 2
    Switch into the sub-account
    Everything from here on happens inside it. If a menu you're told to open isn't there, you're still in the agency view.
  3. 3
    Set timezone to Europe/Dublin
    Settings → Business Profile
    Do this before you create a single calendar or wait step. Changing it later means rebuilding them.
  4. 4
    Fill in business name, address and phone
    These appear in email footers and are needed for sending compliance.
    Settings → Business Profile
  5. 5
    Add your sending subdomain
    Use mail.relier.ie, not the root domain.
    Settings → Email Services → Dedicated Domain
    If deliverability ever tanks, your main domain survives it.
  6. 6
    Add the SPF, DKIM and DMARC records at your registrar
    HighLevel gives you the exact records. Paste them into your DNS.
  7. 7
    Wait for all three records to verify green
    Usually minutes, sometimes hours. Do not send a single email until they're green.
  8. 8
    Buy your phone number
    The one you tested at step 0.
    Settings → Phone Numbers → Add Number
  9. 9
    Send one test SMS to your own mobile and reply to it
    Confirm the reply appears under Conversations. This is the check that everything else assumes.
  10. 10
    Open Custom Values
    Settings → Custom Values
  11. 11
    Create these nine custom values
    Leave the link ones blank for now — you'll fill them in as you create each asset.
    Name → value
    agency_name              RELIER
rep_name                 Ryan
rep_mobile               your mobile
vsl_link                 (fill at step 44)
sales_calendar_link      (fill at step 39)
onboarding_calendar_link (fill at step 43)
onboarding_form_link     (fill at step 35)
reschedule_link          (fill at step 39)
support_email            your email
  12. 12
    Connect Facebook and Instagram
    So DMs and lead-form leads land in Conversations.
    Settings → Integrations
  13. 13
    Set your business hours
    Include 7–9am and 5–7pm. Trade owners are on site 9 to 5.
    Settings → Business Profile
  14. 14
    Add yourself as a user with a real mobile number
    Internal notifications need somewhere to go.
    Settings → My Staff
