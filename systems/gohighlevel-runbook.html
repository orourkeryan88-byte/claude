The three systems, built step by step

Your account already exists, so this starts with an eight-point check and then goes straight into the systems. Each one is self-contained — build it, test it, turn it on, then start the next. Every email and SMS is inline where you paste it.

Pre-flight — check, don't build

P1–P8 · ~15 min

You said HighLevel is set up. These are the eight things the systems below depend on. Each has a fix underneath if it's missing.

  1. P1
    Timezone is Europe/Dublin
    Settings → Business Profile
    Fix this before anything else. Every wait step in every workflow below is timezone-relative — set it wrong and you rebuild them all.
  2. P2
    Sending domain shows all records verified green
    Settings → Email Services
    If it's on your root domain rather than a subdomain like mail.relier.ie, move it. Deliverability problems shouldn't be able to touch your main domain.
  3. P3
    You have an SMS-capable number — and replies reach you
    Text your own mobile from it, then reply. The reply must appear under Conversations.
    This is the one that silently breaks everything. If replies don't arrive, buy a UK +44 mobile number instead — Irish handsets treat it normally. If neither works, tell me and I'll rebuild the sequences around email and click-tracking.
  4. P4
    Call forwarding points at your mobile
    Settings → Phone Numbers → edit number → Call Forwarding Number
    So the HighLevel number rings your actual phone and you keep your personal number personal.
  5. P5
    These nine Custom Values exist
    Settings → Custom Values
    Leave the link ones blank — you fill them as you build.
    Create any that are missing
    agency_name              RELIER
rep_name                 Ryan
rep_mobile               your mobile
support_email            your email
vsl_link                 (filled at step 22)
sales_calendar_link      (filled at step 21)
reschedule_link          (filled at step 21)
onboarding_form_link     (filled at step 51)
onboarding_calendar_link (filled at step 53)
    These are global variables. Change the VSL link in one place instead of eleven.
  6. P6
    Facebook and Instagram are connected
    Settings → Integrations
    So DMs land in Conversations. System 1 depends on it.
  7. P7
    You exist as a user with a real mobile number on the record
    Settings → My Staff
    Internal notifications need somewhere to go.
  8. P8
    Business hours include 7–9am and 5–7pm
    Trade owners are on site 9 to 5. Standard office hours will halve your booking rate.

System 1

Lead Generation

Turn strangers into booked calls without a landing page or a form. The DM is the funnel — he says one word, you qualify in conversation, you book him.

POST ADS → DIRECT MESSAGES → APPOINTMENT SETTING → BOOKED CALL

Steps 11–20 · ~1 hr

What HighLevel does here and what it doesn't. It can't run your Meta ads — build those in Ads Manager. What it does is hold the 22-firm prospect list and never let a follow-up slip. That's the part humans fail at, so that's the part you automate.

System 4

Sales — qualify and boost show rates

Stop no-shows and make sure whoever shows is pre-sold. Every step asks for a small action — watch this, reply YES, accept the invite — and each one raises the cost of ghosting.

CONFIRMATION EMAIL → BOOKING PAGE → SMS → 24HR → 1HR → 5MIN → CALL

Steps 21–45 · ~2.5 hrs

A 40% show rate and a 75% show rate are two different businesses at the same ad spend. Build this before System 2 — it's the one that pays immediately.

  1. 21
    Create calendar "Sales Call — 20 min", then copy its booking and reschedule links into custom values
    20 min duration · 15 min gap · 2 hr minimum notice · 10-day booking window.
    Calendars → Calendar Settings → Create Calendar → Simple
    Bookings further out than 10 days no-show far more often.
  2. 22

    Marketing → Trigger Links
    Structure: the problem (feast or famine) → why referrals cap you → what the system is → a real example → what it costs → who it's not for.

  3. 23

System 2

Onboarding — the moment they say YES

Eliminate buyer's remorse. The 48 hours after signing is when doubt creeps in — he's told his wife he's spending €2,000 a month with a lad he met on Instagram. Every hour of silence is an hour for that decision to rot.

INTAKE → CONTRACT → ONBOARDING FORM → CALENDAR → REMINDERS → CALL
