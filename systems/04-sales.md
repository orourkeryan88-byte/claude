# System 4 — Sales: Qualify Leads, Boost Show Rates

```
CONFIRMATION EMAIL → BOOKING PAGE → INITIAL SMS → 24HR → 1HR → 5MIN → CALL
   watch VSL          VSL + proof     confirm      confirm  Zoom   final
   reply YES          + FAQs          time/date    + wins   ready  link
```

**The job of this system:** stop no-shows, and make sure the people who do show are pre-sold and pre-qualified. A 40% show rate and a 75% show rate are two completely different businesses at the same ad spend.

**The mechanism:** every step asks for a small action. Watch this. Reply YES. Accept the invite. Each micro-commitment raises the cost of ghosting. People don't no-show because they lost interest — they no-show because nothing happened between booking and the call to make it feel real.

## Step 1 — Confirmation Email (sends instantly on booking)

**Subject:** You're booked in for {{Day}} at {{Time}} — two quick things

> {{FirstName}},
>
> You're down for **{{Day}} {{Date}} at {{Time}}**. Zoom link is in the calendar invite.
>
> Two things before we talk, both quick:
>
> **1. Watch this — 6 minutes.** {{VSL_LINK}}
> It's me walking through exactly what I'd build for an attic firm and what it costs. If you watch it, our call goes from a sales pitch to a proper conversation about your business. If you don't, we'll spend the first fifteen minutes on things this video already answers.
>
> **2. Accept the calendar invite** so it's in your diary, not just mine.
>
> And reply **YES** to this email so I know you got it.
>
> One thing I'd ask: if something comes up and you can't make it, just tell me. I'd genuinely rather you moved it than left me sitting on Zoom. Here's the link to move it: {{RESCHEDULE_LINK}}
>
> Ryan
> RELIER

**Why "reply YES" works:** it's a two-second action that converts a passive booking into an active commitment, and it tells you who's engaged. Anyone who hasn't replied YES by the 24-hour mark is your no-show risk — call them.

**Why the reschedule link is prominent:** offering the exit reduces the no-show rate. A moved call is a live opportunity; a no-show is a dead one. Never hide the reschedule option.

## Step 2 — The Booking Page

They see this *before* they book. It does the pre-selling.

**Above the fold**
- Headline: "20 minutes. I'll show you exactly what I'd run for your firm."
- Subhead: "No deck, no pressure. If it's not a fit I'll tell you on the call."
- The calendar embed, immediately visible — never below the fold

**Below the calendar, in this order**
1. **The VSL — 6 minutes.** Face to camera. Structure: the problem (feast/famine) → why referrals cap you → what the system actually is → a real example → what it costs → who it's not for.
2. **Case studies.** Once you have them: firm name, before, after, timeframe, in the owner's own words. Before you have them, replace this block with "I'm building my first Irish case studies right now, which is why the first two firms get it at cost" — honesty converts better than a fake logo wall.
3. **FAQs — answer the objections before the call.**

**The seven FAQs to include**
- What does it cost? *(Answer with a real range. Refusing to say costs you more calls than the number does.)*
- How long before I see leads?
- Do I need to be on camera?
- What if I can't handle the extra work?
- I've tried Facebook ads before and it didn't work — why is this different?
- Am I tied into a contract?
- Do you work with my competitors? *(One firm per area. Say it here.)*

## Step 3 — Initial SMS (sends 5 minutes after booking)

> Ryan from RELIER here — got you down for {{Day}} at {{Time}}. Sent you an email with a short video, worth a watch before we talk. Any bother, just text this number back.

Short. From a real number they can reply to. This is the message that makes you a person rather than a booking system.

>
> Quick confirm for tomorrow at {{Time}}.
>

>

>
> Reply Y to confirm, or move it here if tomorrow's gone pear-shaped: {{RESCHEDULE_LINK}}

**Do the research before this email.** A specific, local, verifiable fact — pulled that morning — is what turns a maybe into a definite. Generic reminders get ignored; this one gets replies.

## Step 6 — 5-Minute Reminder (SMS)

> Starting now — {{ZOOM_LINK}}. I'm on.

**Why five minutes and not zero:** a trade owner is finishing a call or parking the van. Five minutes gives him time to get to a laptop. Sending at the exact start time means he joins at eight minutes past, if at all.

## The no-show recovery (fires 10 minutes after a missed call)

**SMS:**
> No bother {{FirstName}} — things come up on site. Grab another slot whenever suits: {{RESCHEDULE_LINK}}

**Email, 3 hours later:**
> Missed you today, {{FirstName}} — genuinely no problem. Here's the video I was going to walk you through anyway: {{VSL_LINK}}. If it's useful, book back in here: {{RESCHEDULE_LINK}}. If the timing's wrong, tell me and I'll leave you alone.

**Then day 3, day 10.** Around 30% of no-shows rebook if you make it easy and stay pleasant. Being annoyed costs you a third of your pipeline.

## The call itself (20 minutes, present and close)

| Minutes | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Small talk. Ask about the job they're on. Genuinely. |
| 2–7 | **Diagnose, don't pitch.** "Talk me through what happens when someone rings you." Let him talk. Take notes he can see you taking. |
| 7–10 | Play back what you heard, in his words. "So it's feast or famine and you're spending on Checkatrade leads five other lads got." |
| 10–15 | **Show the maths on his numbers.** Job value, margin, close rate, what a lead can cost. This is the moment the sale happens — not the portfolio. |
| 15–18 | The offer. One package, one price, one start date. Do not present three tiers. |
| 18–20 | Close: "Do you want to go ahead?" Then stop talking. |

**The close everyone gets wrong.** After you ask, say nothing. The silence is uncomfortable for about four seconds and then he answers. Fill it and you've talked yourself out of the sale.

**When he says "I'll have a think":**
> Course. Can I ask — is it the money, the timing, or you're not sure it'll work?

Then handle the actual objection rather than the polite one. If he says timing, book the specific follow-up date on the call, before you hang up.

**The moment he says yes → System 2 fires.** See `02-onboarding.md`.

## Show-rate benchmarks

| Stage | Poor | Good | What to fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Booked → showed | under 50% | 75%+ | The reminder ladder isn't built or isn't sending |
| Replied YES to confirmation | under 40% | 70%+ | The confirmation email is too long or has no ask |
| VSL watched before call | under 25% | 50%+ | The video is too long — cut to 6 minutes |
| Showed → closed | under 15% | 25–35% | You're pitching before diagnosing |
