RELIER · System 1 · No ads version

Getting calls booked with cold outreach

No ad spend. No landing page. You have 22 firms and a screen recorder. This is how you turn that into booked calls.

Part 1

How it works

Just reading · 2 min

You record a short video about their business. You send it. You ask for nothing.

Most of them ignore it. Some reply. You chat to the ones who reply, work out if they're any good, and book a call.

Then you follow up with the quiet ones on a schedule, so nobody gets forgotten.

The one rule

You send these one at a time, from your own inbox, by hand.

Do not use HighLevel to blast 22 emails at once. It looks like spam, it lands in junk, and it damages your domain so your real emails stop arriving too. HighLevel's job here is only to remind you who to chase and when.

Part 2

Set it up in HighLevel

14 steps · about 30 minutes · one time only

This builds the thing that tells you who to contact each day. You only do this once.

  1. 1
    On the left menu, click Contacts.
  2. 2
    Top right, click Import Contacts. Upload your prospect list.
    A screen asking you to match up the columns from your file.
  3. 3
    Match the columns: name, phone, email, and put the trade in Trade Type.
  4. 4
    Before you finish, find the box that lets you add a tag. Type src-outbound.
    This tag is the doorbell. Everything else runs off it, so don't skip it.
  5. 5
    Finish the import. Check your 22 people are there.
  6. 6
    Left menu → Automation. Top tabs → Workflows. Then + Create Workflow → Start from Scratch.
  7. 7
    Rename it, top left.
    Name
    Outreach Follow Ups
  8. 8
    Click Add New Trigger. Search tag. Click Contact Tag.
  9. 9
    Set it to Added, and pick the tag src-outbound. Save Trigger.
    Your doorbell is now: "whenever someone gets that sticker."
  10. 10
    Click +. Search task. Click Create Task. Fill it in like this and save.
    Task 1 — due today
    Title:  Record + send video — {{contact.company_name}}
Due:    0 days
Assign: you
  11. 11
    Now add four more, each with a Wait box in between.
    The rest of the chain
    Wait 3 days
Task:  "Nudge — did that video come through?"

Wait 7 days
Task:  "Second video, different angle"

Wait 14 days
Task:  "Ring them — mid morning, between jobs"

Wait 21 days
Remove Tag: src-outbound
Add Tag:    long-list
    Use Time Delay for these waits, not Appointment Time. There's no appointment involved here, so counting from today is exactly right.
  12. 12
    Now the important bit. Click + and add a Goal Event.
    Set it to: tag stage-booked added → then end the workflow.
    Don't skip this one. Without it, someone who books a call on day 4 still gets a "ring them" reminder on day 24. Chasing a man you've already signed makes you look like you're not paying attention.

    Top right, flip Draft to Publish. Click Save.
  14. 14
    Go to Contacts, open one person, and check a task appeared for them.
    A task in your list saying "Record + send video — [their name]". If it's there, it works.
That's the setup done

HighLevel will now hand you a list of who to contact, every day, forever. You never have to remember anyone again.

The rest of this page is what you actually say. That part is the work.

Part 3

The video

90 seconds · this is the whole system

Free tool: Loom. Install it, click record, share your screen, talk. It gives you a link when you stop.

What to have open before you hit record
Tab 1
Their website or Facebook page
Tab 2
The Meta Ad Library, searched for their trade in a nearby townfacebook.com/ads/library — set country to Ireland, type "attic conversions"
What to say
0:00
"Hi Dave, Ryan here. I was having a look at your site this morning."Show their site. Use his actual name and his actual business.
0:15
"There's a crowd over in Swords running four ads right now. Here they are. You're running none."Switch to the Ad Library tab. Show them.
0:45
"The one thing I'd change on your side is this…"Pick ONE thing. Their price isn't shown. Their photos are buried. Their form asks for too much. One thing, not five.
1:15
"No pitch, I just thought it might be useful. If you want the other two things I spotted, say the word."Then stop. Don't ask for a call.
Why you don't ask for a call

He's braced for a pitch. The second you ask for something, he stops watching and deletes it.

Give it away for free and a few of them will ask you what you do. Those ones are already sold.

Part 4

What you send

Copy these · swap the names

Day 0 — the first email

Send it from your normal inbox. The subject line is just his company name. Nothing else.

First email
Subject: Attic Masters

Dave — made you a 90-second video about your site, no pitch attached.

[paste your Loom link]

If it's useful I'll send the other two things I spotted. If not,
ignore me and I won't chase.

Ryan

Leave "I won't chase" in. It's the line that gets replies, because it removes the exact thing he's dreading. And it's true — you don't chase, you send a different video instead.

Send the same thing as an Instagram DM too, if they have one. Takes ten seconds and it often lands better than email.

Day 3 — the nudge

Nudge
Did that come through alright, Dave?

That's the whole message. Reply to your own email so the video is underneath it.

Day 10 — a second video

New recording, different angle. Do not mention the first one.

Second video email
Subject: One more thing on Attic Masters

Dave — spotted something else on your Google listing. 60 seconds:

[paste link]

Ryan

Day 24 — ring him

Mid-morning, between 10 and 11. He's between jobs and not on a roof.

What to say on the phone
"Dave? Ryan here, I sent you a couple of videos about your website —
not sure if they landed. I won't keep you. Are you taking on
much new work at the minute, or are you flat out?"

Then listen. That question does the qualifying for you.

Part 5

When someone replies

This is where it gets real

Reply fast. Within an hour if you can. Then ask these four questions — one at a time, waiting for each answer.

The four questions
1. How many jobs a month are you doing now, and how many
   could you handle?

2. What's an average job for you, roughly?

3. Where's the work coming from at the moment?

4. When someone rings, who picks up?

Never paste all four at once. A list of questions looks like a form and gets ignored.

What his answers mean

He saysYou do
"Booked out, couldn't take more"Don't sell. Ask when his diary frees up, write it down, come back then.
Average job under €6,000Tell him straight it won't pay for itself, and move on. He'll remember you for that.
"All word of mouth"This is the good one. Book him.
"Tried Facebook ads, waste of money"Ask what he ran and for how long. Usually two weeks with nothing tracked. Easy to explain.
"The wife takes the calls"Fine. Someone answers. That's what matters.
"I've got someone doing that"Ask what he's getting from them. Either he's happy — leave it — or you've just found your way in.

Booking the call

The ask
Right, that all stacks up. I'd rather show you than type it —
20 minutes on a call, I'll walk you through what I'd run for you
specifically and you can tell me if it's mad. No pitch deck, no
pressure. Does Thursday morning suit?
Give him two times, not a link

"Thursday morning or Friday afternoon?" works. A calendar link does not — he's on a phone, on a site, and he won't open it.

Once he says a time out loud, then send the booking link to lock it in. That's what starts your reminder system.

Last thing: in HighLevel, put the tag stage-booked on him. That switches off his follow-up reminders automatically.

Part 6

Your actual week

This is the bit that decides whether it works

The system doesn't fail because the videos are bad. It fails because people do four of them and stop.

WhenWhat
Every morning
8:00–9:00		Open your task list in HighLevel. Do whatever it tells you. Usually three videos, or a nudge and a phone call.
Through the dayReply to anyone who replied. Within the hour.
FridayCount what you sent. Write the number down.

The numbers to expect

ThingAim for
Videos sent per week15
Replies3 out of every 15
Calls booked1 out of every 4 who reply
They turn up7 out of 10 (that's System 4's job)
They sign1 in 4 of the ones who turn up

Work that through and 22 firms, done properly, is roughly your first client. Which is why you don't add a 23rd name until all 22 have had the full run.

If you're getting no replies at all

Fewer than 1 in 15 replying means the message is wrong, not the market.

Nine times out of ten it's because the video was about you instead of about them. Watch your last one back. If you say "I" more than you say their name, that's the problem.
