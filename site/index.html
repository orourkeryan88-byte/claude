Questions
Do I need a new phone number? +
No. You keep your existing number. You just forward calls you don't answer to your AI number — your customers dial exactly what they always have.
Will it sound robotic? +
No — it uses a natural human voice, and you choose which one. Most callers can't tell. It's warm, it's brief, and it never puts anyone on hold.
What if it can't handle a call? +
It's built to never dead-end. If it's unsure, it takes a message; if it's an emergency or the caller wants a person, it transfers to you. Nothing is ever lost.
How fast can I go live? +
Usually within a day. We build it for your business, connect your calendar, and hand you the number and the app — done for you.
How much is it, really? +
€350 to set up and €80 a month, everything included. Cheaper than one missed job — and far cheaper than a wage.
