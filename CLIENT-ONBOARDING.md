# Your new AI Receptionist — getting started

Welcome! Your 24/7 AI receptionist is ready. It answers every call to your business, takes the caller's details, books them in, and **texts you the lead straight away** — so you never lose a job to a missed call again.

This page covers the **one thing you need to do**: forward your calls. It takes about 2 minutes.

---

## How it works

- Your existing business number **stays exactly the same**. Customers ring it like always.
- When you can't pick up (you're on a job, it's after hours, the line's busy), the call **forwards** to your AI receptionist.
- The receptionist answers in your business's name, helps the caller, and **texts you their name, number and what they need.**
- You ring them back when you're free — or they're already booked in.

---

## The one step: turn on call forwarding

Pick the option that suits you.

### Option 1 — Forward only when you don't answer (recommended)
This way you still answer calls yourself when you can, and the AI only catches the ones you'd otherwise miss.

Dial this from your business phone (works on most Irish networks):

- **Forward when no answer:** dial `**61*<AI-NUMBER>#` then press call
- **Forward when busy:** dial `**67*<AI-NUMBER>#` then press call
- **Forward when unreachable (off/no signal):** dial `**62*<AI-NUMBER>#` then press call

Replace `<AI-NUMBER>` with the number we gave you (starts with +353…).

To **turn it off** later: dial `##61#`, `##67#`, `##62#`.

### Option 2 — Forward every call to the AI
If you want the AI to answer everything (great for after-hours or busy periods):

- Dial `**21*<AI-NUMBER>#` then call. Turn off with `##21#`.

> **Not sure / different network?** Just ring us and we'll set it up with you on the phone in 2 minutes.

## What a lead text looks like

When someone calls and the AI takes their details, you'll get a text like this:

```
🔔 NEW LEAD — [Your Business]
Name: Sarah Byrne
Phone: 087 555 0192
Job: Leak under kitchen sink
When: Today, 2–4pm
⚠ Marked URGENT
```

Urgent jobs (leaks, no heat, emergencies) are flagged so you can jump on them first.
