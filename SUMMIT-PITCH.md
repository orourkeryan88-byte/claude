# Summit pitch script — AI Receptionist

Keep this open on your phone. The whole pitch is **under 3 minutes**. Demo first, talk second.

---

## The 20-second opener (stop them walking past)

> "Quick question — how many calls a week does your business miss when you're busy or closed? … Every one of those is a job that probably went to a competitor. Can I show you something that fixes that in 30 seconds?"

Then open the demo on your laptop/phone.

---

## The demo (your weapon — let it do the talking)

1. In **Demo settings**, pick their **industry** and type **their business name**.
2. Hit **▶ Play live demo call**.
3. Stay quiet. Let them hear it answer *as their business*, take the caller's details, book the job, and watch the **lead text** appear.
4. One line when it finishes:
   > "That just answered a call you'd have missed, booked the job, and texted you the lead — at 2am if it had to. It never sleeps, never calls in sick, and handles ten calls at once."

If they're engaged, hit **🎙 Talk to it** and let *them* talk to it. That's the moment they buy.

---

## The money slide (scroll to the calculator)

1. Scroll to **"How much are missed calls costing you?"**
2. Slide it to *their* real numbers — missed calls a week, average job value.
3. Point at the red number:
   > "That's what's walking out the door every month. This costs ninety-nine. You do the maths."

---

## The close

> "I can have this answering your phone, in your business's name, by the end of the week. First two weeks are free — if it doesn't catch you jobs, you cancel and pay nothing. Want me to set it up?"

If yes → get their **business name, what they do, opening hours, and the number leads should be texted to.** That's all you need to go live (see `SETUP-GUIDE.md` Part B).

If "let me think" → "No bother — let me take your number and I'll text you the live demo link so you can play with it yourself." (Then follow up tomorrow.)

---

## Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Won't it sound like a robot?" | "Have a listen —" (play the demo / hand them the mic). "Most callers don't realise. And it's your name on the greeting." |
| "I'd rather answer myself." | "You still can. It only picks up the calls you'd otherwise miss — after hours, on a job, line busy. It's a safety net, not a replacement." |
| "What if it can't answer something?" | "It takes a message and texts you straight away, so you ring them back. You never lose the lead." |
| "Too expensive." | "It's a fraction of a receptionist's wage, and it catches calls 24/7. One recovered job a month usually pays for the year." |
| "I already have voicemail." | "How many people actually leave one? Most just ring the next plumber. This *talks* to them and books them in." |
| "Is my data safe?" | "Yes — calls go through secure platforms, leads come straight to your phone. Nothing's shared." |
